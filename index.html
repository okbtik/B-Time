♟ Chess

Traditional 8×8 battlefield. Pawns move forward 1 square (or 2 on their initial move) and capture diagonally. Castling and pawn promotion are supported. Checkmate the opposing King to win!

帥 Xiangqi (Chinese Chess)

Played on the 9×10 intersections. The General (帥/將) and Advisors (仕/士) must stay within their 3×3 Nine Palace. Elephants (相/象) cannot cross the central River and can be blocked by adjacent pieces. Horses (傌/馬) step one point orthogonally and one diagonally, subject to blocking. Cannons (炮/砲) jump exactly one piece to capture. Soldiers (兵/卒) gain horizontal movement after crossing the river.

王 Shogi (Japanese Chess)

Played on a 9×9 grid. Pieces you capture from your opponent are added to your Komadai (hand reserve) and can be dropped back onto any empty square as your own! Pieces can promote upon reaching the enemy's 3-row camp. (Special restrictions: Two pawns in the same file / Nifu and dropping a pawn to checkmate / Uchifuzume are illegal).
